# Marathon Training Nutrition Plan

## Daily Calorie Target
**~2,800-3,200 calories** (depending on training intensity/volume)
- BMR: ~1,700 cal | Add 65-90% for marathon training

## Macronutrient Distribution

| Macro | Daily | % of Calories | Purpose |
|-------|-------|---|---|
| **Carbs** | 450-550g | 55-60% | Fuel & glycogen stores |
| **Protein** | 110-135g | 14-16% | Recovery & adaptation |
| **Fat** | 80-90g | 25-30% | Hormonal health & sustained energy |

## Key Nutrition Strategies

### **Carbohydrate Loading (3-7 days pre-race)**
- Increase to 70-90g carbs/kg body weight
- Taper training volume while maintaining intensity
- Choose easily digestible carbs

### **Daily Meal Framework**

**Breakfast** (500-600 cal)
- Oatmeal + banana + honey + Greek yogurt, or
- Whole grain toast + eggs + berries

**Mid-Morning Snack** (200 cal)
- Banana + almond butter or sports drink + granola

**Lunch** (700-800 cal)
- Lean protein (chicken/fish) + brown rice/quinoa + vegetables + olive oil

**Pre-Workout Snack** (200-300 cal, 60-90min before)
- White bread + jam, or energy gel + water

**Post-Workout** (400-500 cal, within 30-60min)
- Chocolate milk, or protein + carbs (chicken sandwich, pasta)

**Dinner** (600-700 cal)
- Lean protein + sweet potato + vegetables

**Evening Snack** (100-200 cal, if needed)
- Greek yogurt + berries

### **Hydration**
- **Daily**: 3-4L water minimum
- **During training**: 500-750mL/hour (with electrolytes on runs >90min)
- **Post-workout**: 150% of fluid lost, over 4-6 hours

### **Race Week Specifics**
- Prioritize hydration & sodium intake
- Stick to familiar foods (nothing new!)
- Reduce fiber 24-48h before race
